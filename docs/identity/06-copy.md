# 6. Copy

This is the published text of the site, section by section. The source of
truth is the code (`src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/approach/page.tsx`, and the
components they use); this document is the readable copy deck.

## Rules the copy follows

1. **Nothing invented.** No clients, results, recoveries, testimonials,
   partnerships, certifications, team members, product capabilities, or
   financial statistics. The only figures on the site belong to one worked
   example that is labelled hypothetical wherever it appears.
2. **Three amounts, never blended.** Potential exposure is not a validated
   claim. A validated claim is not a realized outcome. Each figure is named
   for what it is, beside the figure.
3. **No guarantees.** The question "Do you guarantee recoveries or savings?"
   is answered "No."
4. **The customer keeps authority.** Supplier relationships, purchasing
   approvals, and commercial decisions stay with the customer. Supplier
   contact happens only with authorization.
5. **No software is described.** Kordran sells investigation and
   follow-through, so nothing is presented as a product or platform.
6. **Plain sentences.** Short, declarative, concrete. No superlatives.

## Approach

### Hero

- **Label:** Approach
- **Headline:** From supplier records to commercial resolution.
- **Lede:** A focused process for investigating pricing discrepancies,
  validating financial exposure, and supporting corrective action. It begins
  with a defined problem and ends with a confirmed result.
- **Action:** Discuss a specific issue
- **01 Records.** Quotations, agreements, orders, and invoices describe the
  same purchase. They do not always agree.
- **02 Reconciliation.** Set against each other, the records show where a
  billed price stands apart from the agreed one.
- **03 Resolution.** A supported difference becomes an authorized
  correction, and the correction is confirmed.
- **Index:** 01 Fit · 02 Evidence · 03 Workflow · 04 Measurement ·
  05 Deliverables · 06 Control

### 01 Fit

- **Heading:** Start with a defined problem.
- **Lede:** Kordran is deliberately specialized. A defined issue keeps the
  investigation focused and commercially useful.
- **Suitable issues**
  - A supplier's billed price does not appear to match agreed terms.
  - A price increase is difficult to reconcile with the commercial
    agreement.
  - A contractual surcharge may have been applied incorrectly.
  - Pricing terms changed, but the purchasing system may not reflect the
    revision.
  - A financially material supplier dispute lacks a clear evidence trail.
- **Not an initial fit**
  - General procurement outsourcing.
  - Full enterprise supply-chain transformation.
  - Broad strategic sourcing without a specific economic problem.
  - Formal legal or customs advice.
  - Requests for guaranteed recovery results.

### 02 Evidence

- **Heading:** Follow the pricing evidence.
- **Lede:** A supplier pricing issue often spans several records. We
  reconcile the relevant sources to establish the applicable terms and the
  financial differences.

| # | Record | What it establishes |
| - | ------ | ------------------- |
| 01 | Supplier quotation | The price a supplier offered, the quantities it covered, and how long the offer stood. |
| 02 | Pricing agreement | The agreed price, its effective dates, and any terms for escalation or surcharges. |
| 03 | Contract amendment | What changed after signing, and the date the change took effect. |
| 04 | Approved price change | Which revisions your company actually accepted, and from when. |
| 05 | Purchase order | What was ordered, in what quantity, and at what stated price. |
| 06 | Supplier invoice | What was billed. This is the record the others are tested against. |

- **Note:** Purchasing history and relevant internal correspondence add
  context. An initial review does not require a full system integration.
  The records depend on the issue.

### 03 Workflow

- **Heading:** A defined path to resolution.
- **Lede:** Each step has work we do and a part that stays with you.

| Step | Kordran | Your part |
| ---- | ------- | --------- |
| 01 Scope | Identify the supplier, the commercial issue, the affected transactions, and the evidence required. | Choose the supplier and the issue. Approve the scope. |
| 02 Reconcile | Compare the relevant records and quantify the discrepancies they show. | Provide the selected records through the agreed process. |
| 03 Validate | Test the difference against terms, dates, amendments, exceptions, and supporting information. | Confirm context and any exceptions you already know about. |
| 04 Resolve | Prepare an evidence-backed course of action and support authorized supplier discussions where appropriate. | Decide what to pursue. Authorize any contact with the supplier. |
| 05 Verify | Confirm accepted corrections, credits applied, or other measurable outcomes. | Confirm what was actually received or corrected. |
| 06 Prevent | Recommend practical controls that reduce the chance of the same issue recurring. | Decide which controls to adopt. |

- **Note:** Not every engagement includes negotiation, a credit, or a
  control change. The work follows the findings and the agreed scope.

### 04 Measurement

- **Heading:** A finding is not a recovery.
- **Lede:** These states stay separate. A negotiated discount is not a cash
  recovery, and an unaccepted claim is not realized value.
- **Sheet header:** Example continued · Machined housing · One square = 100
  units — Hypothetical figures

The homepage chart and the Approach waterfall use this one case.

| State | Figure | Arithmetic | Definition |
| ----- | ------ | ---------- | ---------- |
| 01 Observed | $312,000 | 5,000 × $2.60 × 24 | The difference identified over 24 months. |
| 02 Excluded | −$52,000 | 20,000 × $2.60 | Units bought on separate terms, taken out of the claim. |
| 03 Supported claim | $260,000 | $312,000 − $52,000 | What the records support. Not an accepted credit. |
| 04 If corrected, a year | $156,000 | 5,000 × $2.60 × 12 | Future cost avoidance if volume continues and the unit price is corrected. Not cash already recovered. |

- **Caption:** Hypothetical example for illustration only. Not a Kordran
  customer result.
- **Case copy:**
  - Of the 120,000 units observed, 20,000 were bought on separate terms and
    come out of the claim.
  - The other 100,000 units support a $260,000 claim. Kordran helps pursue a
    credit or a pricing correction. Acceptance is not assumed.
  - If the price returns to $42.00 and buying continues, about $156,000 a
    year is not billed. That is future cost avoidance, not cash already
    recovered.
