# 5. Information architecture

## Pages

| Path | Purpose | Buyer question it answers |
| ---- | ------- | ------------------------- |
| `/` | Positioning, the problem, the scope, the method in brief, common questions, contact | "What is this, is it for me, and how do I start?" |
| `/approach` | How an engagement runs, from records to a confirmed result | "What exactly would you do, what stays with us, and how do you count results?" |
| `/privacy` | Privacy policy | Legal |
| `/terms` | Terms of use | Legal |

Contact is an action, not a page. It is the homepage's last section
(`/#contact`) and the primary button in the header on every page.

### Pages considered and not added

Each candidate was tested against six questions: does it answer a distinct
buyer question, does it hold substantive content not found elsewhere, does it
improve navigation, does it avoid repeating the homepage, can real business
content support it, and is it worth maintaining.

| Candidate | Decision | Reason |
| --------- | -------- | ------ |
| About / Team | Not added | No team content may be invented, and none was supplied. |
| Case studies / Results | Not added | No client results exist to publish. The worked example is labelled hypothetical and lives where it is needed. |
| Industries | Not added | Would repeat "industrial manufacturers" with no supporting detail. |
| Pricing / Engagement terms | Not added | A commercial decision for Kordran. The site says only that engagements are set by a separate written agreement. |
| Questions (own page) | Not added | Seven answers fit on the homepage, directly before the contact form, where they remove hesitation. |
| Insights / Blog | Not added | No content exists. An empty section would undermine the site. |

## Navigation

- **Header:** logo (home), Approach, Questions (`/#questions`), and the
  primary action "Discuss a supplier issue" (`/#contact`). The current page
  is marked with a small teal square under its link.
- **Phone:** logo and a Menu button. The menu holds Home, Approach,
  Questions, and the primary action. Escape closes it and returns focus to
  the button; the rest of the page is inert while it is open.
- **Footer:** Home, Approach, Questions, Contact; email, LinkedIn, X;
  Privacy and Terms.
- **Approach page index:** six chips under the hero jump to each section.
- **Skip link:** "Skip to content" is the first focusable element.

The previous header carried both a Contact link and a contact button. The
link was removed and replaced by Questions, which is the more useful second
destination for a buyer who is not ready to write.

the previous site still land somewhere sensible.

## Rhythm

The two pages alternate three grounds so that no two adjacent sections look
the same, and so the dark band appears once per page:

- **Paper** (`--paper`): hero, problem, purpose, contact.
- **Sheet** (`--sheet`): what we examine, questions, the lighter Approach
  sections.
- **Deep** (`--deep`): intelligence + execution on the homepage; working
  relationship on Approach.

Each page ends by rising into the painting and settling into the footer.
