# 2. Creative strategy

## Thesis

**Kordran makes a commercial difference measurable, then sees it corrected.**
The identity should look like the instrument that does the measuring.

A surface plate is the flat reference a machinist checks parts against.
Anything that does not sit flush is out of tolerance, and the gap can be
measured. Kordran does the same thing with supplier pricing: the agreement is
the reference, each invoice line is checked against it, and what stands proud
is the finding. The identity takes that literally.

## What a visitor should understand, in order

1. **What this is.** Supplier costs, verified and resolved, for industrial
   manufacturers. Stated in the headline and the sentence under it.
2. **What the picture means.** A grid of records. Most are flush. Some stand
   up, in the brand color. The legend says "Billed as agreed" and "Billed
   above agreement".
3. **Why it matters.** $2.00 on one part, across 18,000 units, is $36,000.
4. **Why Kordran is careful.** That figure is exposure, not recovery. The
   site says so beside the number.
5. **What happens next.** Verify, resolve, prevent. Start with one supplier.

## How it differs

| Compared with | They look like | Kordran instead |
| ------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Traditional industrial consulting | Stock plant photography, navy and grey, long service lists | No stock imagery. One painting. A drawn system. One narrow offer stated plainly. |
| Generic B2B SaaS | Gradient hero, dashboard screenshot, logo wall, feature grid | No software is shown because none is sold. Figures are drawings of records, not interface mockups. No proof points are invented. |
| High-end fintech | Serif headlines, hairline rules, dark bands, wealth cues | A sans with engineering proportions, mono labels, paper and ink. Money appears as arithmetic a buyer can check, not as lifestyle. |
| Experimental creative sites | The canvas is the page; content waits for it | The canvas is an enhancement over a complete server-rendered drawing. The page is fully readable with scripting, WebGL, or motion turned off. |

## Principles

1. **Draw the work.** Every figure depicts records being compared. No
   abstract "technology" imagery.
2. **One projection.** Everything dimensional uses the same axonometric
   angle, the same three face tones, and the same tile unit. The two flat
   views, the unit grid (plan) and the price-line profile (elevation), are
   views of that same tile.
3. **Teal means difference.** The accent is reserved for the thing Kordran
   finds, for the primary action, and for the mark. It is not decoration.
4. **Say less, mean it.** Short sentences. No claims that cannot be
   supported. Exposure, validated claim, and realized outcome are never
   blended.
5. **The painting is the only warmth.** Paper, ink, and teal everywhere
   else, so the painting lands.
6. **Calm at rest.** The plate moves slowly and stops when it is off screen.
   Nothing loops for attention.

## Brand continuity

Kept from the previous site: the logo, the teal (`#1C3D57`, a blue-leaning
teal sampled from the mark), Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono, the headline and
supporting sentence, the worked example figures, and the Industrial Wash
painting at the foot of the page.

Changed: the ground moved from white to warm paper; the hero graphic moved
from a wireframe lattice to the surface plate; the painting moved from under
a dark scrim to a full-width close that the page rises into; figures were
redrawn as one system; the header lost its redundant Contact link.

## What the identity can grow into

The tile is a unit that scales. A case note can show its own plate. A
supplier review can be a row. A report cover can be a crop of the grid. Print
and slides can use the same projection with no change of tools, because the
drawing is plain geometry in three flat tones.
